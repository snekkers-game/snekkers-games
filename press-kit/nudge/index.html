02 / THE GAME

Descriptions

Pitch

An action RPG with tower defense: recover crystals, collect loot, craft equipment, and build your skills to help restore a girl’s suppressed memories.

Description

Take control of Nudge and fight enemy waves while positioning towers to support your defense. Discover loot and unique items, or craft your own equipment with Exile-inspired mechanics. As you level up, expand a distinctive Skill Grid and combine newly discovered skills into different builds. Recover crystals along the way to help restore a girl’s suppressed memories.

Features

  • Combine direct action RPG combat with strategic tower placement against waves of enemies.
  • Collect loot and unique items, and craft equipment using Exile-inspired systems.
  • Unlock skills and experiment with builds in a Skill Grid that grows as you level up.
  • Play solo with full controller support, including Xbox and PlayStation controllers.
  • English and Simplified Chinese interfaces and subtitles.
